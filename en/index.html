Twinkle Kitchen Play

Gather, wash, prepare, and cook food for 15 animal friends. Share one bite at a time.

Rainbow Noodles, Pizza, and Pancakes are free without play limits. One optional purchase adds the current 12 extra recipes, for 15 in total. There is no subscription or automatic renewal; future recipes may be separate.

The app supports Korean, English, Japanese, and German. It uses the app language selected on the device and an available Apple system voice for spoken guidance.

No registration, advertising, or third-party tracking. This is pretend cooking on a screen. Adults should handle real knives and hot cooking tools.
