Twinkle Kitchen Play

Welcome to Twinkle Kitchen Play! Gather, wash, and prepare ingredients to make a meal for an animal friend.

All 15 recipes are free. Stretch noodles, roll gimbap, fold dumplings, and make pizza, pancakes, waffles and more.

Share your finished dish one bite at a time. The first shared bite creates a memory card in the friends album on your device.

Adjust voice guidance, music, effects and vibration, or choose button controls. Supports Korean, English, Japanese and German. No registration, advertising or third-party tracking.

This is pretend cooking on a screen. Adults should handle real knives, heat and hot cooking tools.
