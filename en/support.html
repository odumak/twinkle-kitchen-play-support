How to play

Drag ingredients, wash off the dirt, and cut along dotted lines. Wait for your friend to finish chewing before sharing another bite.

Use buttons instead of dragging

Turn on Cook with buttons in Kitchen Settings. It works at any text size.

Language and sound

Select a supported app language in your device settings. Check the app's sound, voice, effects and music settings and the device volume. Spoken guidance requires an available Apple voice for the selected language; the app does not substitute a different language. The cooking screen's speaker button toggles all sound together. To repeat an instruction, use the instruction action in the ellipsis menu.

Find all the recipes

Tap Cook Together, then scroll the food pictures sideways to choose a recipe. In the large-text layout, scroll the recipe list up and down.

Purchases and restores

Open For parents on the welcome screen or recipe list and complete the parent check. The Parent Area lists the fixed 12 extra recipes and the actual App Store price. Restore with the same App Store account and an internet connection. Free and previously verified purchased recipes work offline. Clearing the cooking count is not a refund.
