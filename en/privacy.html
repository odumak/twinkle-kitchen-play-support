Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-02

Personal information we collect

Twinkle Kitchen Play works without registration. The app does not request names, contact details, dates of birth, location, photos, voice recordings or advertising identifiers, or send them to a developer server.

Information stored on your device

Sound, music, spoken guidance, vibration and button-control preferences, along with the number of completed dishes, are stored on the device. An unfinished cooking session is not kept on a server or synced after the app closes. The device also remembers whether the introductory guide has been dismissed so it is not repeated. The friends album stores the type of dish first shared, its animal friend and artwork version on the device. Memory cards use artwork included in the app, without taking photos or uploading anything.

Transfers and third parties

The app includes no advertising, third-party analytics, account server or user tracking. Cooking images and music are bundled in the app. Spoken guidance reads instructions using Apple system voices; it does not record or recognize the user’s voice.

In-app purchases and restoring purchases

Rainbow Noodles, Pizza and Pancakes are free. Twelve extra recipes are available as a one-time purchase in the Parent Area. Apple processes payments and purchase account information. The app checks Apple-verified transactions on the device to determine access and does not send them to a developer server. The developer does not receive card details or App Store account passwords. Purchases and restores may require internet access and Apple authentication. Deleting the app or resetting the completed-dish count does not delete Apple purchase history or issue a refund.

Retention and deletion

Preferences and completed-dish counts are kept on the device. The count can be cleared in the Parent Area. Deleting the app removes its local data on that device. Data included in device backups and restores depends on Apple backup settings; the developer cannot access those backups. The friends album can be deleted separately in the parent area. Deleting the completed-dish count does not delete the album.

Children’s privacy

Children are not asked to enter personal information or create an account. The Parent Area opens after a verification question. Links to external support pages and email apps are available only there. This check does not obtain consent to collect personal information.

Parent inquiries

When a parent sends an inquiry through an external email app, the operator may see the sender’s email address and message to respond. Please do not include children’s names, photos, contact details or other personal information. Parents may request deletion of inquiry records through the support email in the Parent Area. Unnecessary personal information should be deleted without undue delay after handling the inquiry.

Changes to this notice

If information handling changes, this notice and App Store privacy information will be updated together. Cooking in the app is pretend play and does not replace instructions for cooking real food.
