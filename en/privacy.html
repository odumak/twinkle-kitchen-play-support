Privacy Policy

The app does not require an account or send names, dates of birth, location, voice recordings or advertising identifiers to a developer server. It contains no advertising, third-party analytics or user tracking.

Settings, completed cooking counts and the friends album are stored on the device. The counts and album can be deleted separately in the parent area. Deleting the app removes its device data; separate copies may remain in device backups.

If you email us, the operator can see your address and message to reply. Please do not send children’s names, photos or contact details. You may request deletion of correspondence. The email provider’s and website host’s privacy policies also apply.
